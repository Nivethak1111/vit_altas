# Clinical Trial Protocol STUDY-042 (Version 1.0)
**Date:** January 05, 2024  
**Title:** A Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Phase 2 Study of Drug-X (10 mg QD) in Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus

## 1. Study Objectives & Design
This is a multicenter, randomized, double-blind, parallel-group clinical trial evaluating the efficacy and safety of oral Drug-X 10 mg once daily versus placebo.

## 2. Inclusion & Exclusion Criteria
### Inclusion Criteria:
- Male or female aged 18 to 75 years inclusive at the time of screening.
- Diagnosed with Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus with HbA1c between 7.0% and 10.5% inclusive at screening.
- Receiving a stable regimen of Metformin monotherapy (>= 1000 mg daily) for at least 8 weeks prior to Day 1.

### Exclusion Criteria:
- ALT or AST > 2x Upper Limit of Normal (ULN) at screening.
- Positive urine or serum pregnancy test at screening or baseline.
- History of severe cardiovascular disease or acute coronary syndrome within 6 months.

## 3. Investigational Medicinal Product (IMP) & Dosing
- **Active Drug:** 10 mg tablet administered orally once daily in the morning with water.
- **Placebo:** Matching placebo tablet (0 mg active drug) administered orally once daily.
- Any administered dose differing from 10 mg (Active) or 0 mg (Placebo) constitutes a major protocol deviation.

## 4. Visit Schedule & Window
- Scheduled study visits occur at Screening (Day -14 to -1), Day 1 (Baseline), Week 2, Week 4, Week 8, Week 12, Week 16, and Week 24.
- **Allowed Visit Window (Protocol v1.0):** ±7 days around the target study day.

## 5. Concomitant Medications
- **Prohibited Medications:** Systemic glucocorticoids (oral, intravenous, or intramuscular) are prohibited throughout the trial duration.

## 6. Safety Monitoring & Serious Adverse Events (SAEs)
- An Adverse Event (AE) is defined as any untoward medical occurrence.
- An AE is classified as **Serious (SAE)** if it meets any of the following criteria:
  - Results in death
  - Is life-threatening
  - Requires inpatient hospitalization or prolongation of existing hospitalization
  - Results in persistent or significant disability/incapacity
  - Is a congenital anomaly or birth defect
- **Reporting Requirement:** Any SAE must be reported by the Investigator to the Sponsor Safety Desk within 24 hours of site awareness.
- **Hepatic Safety:** Potential Hy's Law candidates (ALT or AST > 3x ULN and Total Bilirubin > 2x ULN within 14 days without cholestasis) mandate immediate study drug interruption and hepatology adjudication.
