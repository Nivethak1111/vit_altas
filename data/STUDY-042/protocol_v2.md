# Clinical Trial Protocol STUDY-042 (Version 2.0 - Amendment 1)
**Date:** May 15, 2024  
**Title:** A Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Phase 2 Study of Drug-X (10 mg QD) in Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus

## Summary of Changes (Amendment 1):
1. **Visit Window Tightening:** Visit window narrowed from ±7 days to **±3 days** around scheduled target days to improve pharmacodynamic data precision.
2. **Renal Safety Exclusion Added:** Due to emerging non-clinical data, patients with serum Creatinine > 1.5 mg/dL at screening are excluded from enrollment.

## 1. Study Objectives & Design
Phase 2 multicenter, randomized trial comparing oral Drug-X 10 mg once daily versus placebo.

## 2. Inclusion & Exclusion Criteria
### Inclusion Criteria:
- Age 18–75 inclusive.
- HbA1c 7.0%–10.5% inclusive.
- Stable Metformin for >= 8 weeks prior to Day 1.

### Exclusion Criteria:
- ALT or AST > 2x ULN at screening.
- **Serum Creatinine > 1.5 mg/dL at screening (New in v2.0).**
- Positive pregnancy test at screening.

## 3. Investigational Medicinal Product (IMP) & Dosing
- Drug: 10 mg QD
- Placebo: 0 mg QD

## 4. Visit Schedule & Window
- **Allowed Visit Window (Protocol v2.0):** **±3 days** from target study day.

## 5. Concomitant Medications
- Systemic glucocorticoids remain prohibited.

## 6. Serious Adverse Events & Hy's Law
- Seriousness criteria unchanged (Death, Life-threatening, Hospitalization, Disability, Congenital).
- 24-hour expedited reporting requirement remains mandatory.
- Hy's Law candidate monitoring unchanged (ALT/AST > 3x ULN + TBIL > 2x ULN in 14 days without cholestasis).
