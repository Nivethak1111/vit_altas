# Statistical Analysis Plan (SAP) STUDY-042
**Protocol Title:** A Phase 2 Study of Drug-X 10 mg in T2DM  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** February 01, 2024  

## 1. Analysis Populations
- **All Randomized Population:** All subjects randomized to study treatment.
- **Safety Population:** All subjects who received at least one dose of investigational medicinal product (Drug-X or Placebo).

## 2. Primary Efficacy Endpoint
Change from baseline in HbA1c at Week 24 evaluated via mixed-effects model for repeated measures (MMRM).

## 3. Safety Endpoints & Cut-Aware Monitoring
Safety summaries will be generated at defined interim data cuts:
- Cut 1 (Month 2 interim)
- Cut 2 (Month 5 interim)
- Cut 3 (Final primary database lock)
Adverse events and laboratory shifts will be evaluated based on the active protocol version applicable at each respective cut.
Retrospective data corrections logged in the audit trail will be applied from the specified correction cut onward.
