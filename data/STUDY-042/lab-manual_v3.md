# Laboratory Services Manual STUDY-042 (Version 3.0)
**Central Laboratory:** Global Clinical Diagnostics Laboratories  
**Effective Date:** August 20, 2024  

## 1. Updated Analytical Platforms
Maintains reference ranges established in v1.0. Reaffirms the mandatory conversion:
- 1 µkat/L = 60 U/L for ALT/AST/ALP enzymatic measurements.

## 2. Critical Safety Alert Flags
Automatic laboratory alerts are dispatched to the Study Sentinel platform whenever:
- ALT or AST > 3x ULN
- Total Bilirubin > 2x ULN
- Any concurrent elevation within 14 calendar days (Potential Hy's Law candidate)
- Serum Creatinine > 1.5 mg/dL at screening
