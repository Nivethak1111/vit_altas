# Clinical Trial Protocol STUDY-042 (Version 3.0 - Amendment 2)
**Date:** August 20, 2024  
**Title:** A Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Phase 2 Study of Drug-X (10 mg QD) in Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus

## Summary of Changes (Amendment 2):
1. **Prohibited Concomitant Medications Expanded:** Concomitant use of **Sulfonylureas** (e.g. Glimepiride, Glipizide, Glyburide) is now strictly prohibited due to potential hypoglycemic risk in combination with Drug-X.
2. Visit window remains ±3 days.
3. Serum Creatinine > 1.5 mg/dL screening exclusion remains active.

## 1. Study Objectives & Design
Phase 2 multicenter, randomized trial comparing oral Drug-X 10 mg once daily versus placebo.

## 2. Inclusion & Exclusion Criteria
- Age 18–75 inclusive.
- HbA1c 7.0%–10.5% inclusive.
- Stable Metformin for >= 8 weeks prior to Day 1.
- Hepatic exclusion: ALT or AST > 2x ULN at screening.
- Renal exclusion: Serum Creatinine > 1.5 mg/dL at screening.
- Pregnancy exclusion.

## 3. Investigational Medicinal Product (IMP) & Dosing
- Drug: 10 mg QD
- Placebo: 0 mg QD

## 4. Visit Schedule & Window
- Allowed Visit Window: **±3 days** from target study day.

## 5. Concomitant Medications
- **Prohibited:** Systemic glucocorticoids.
- **Prohibited (New in v3.0):** **Sulfonylureas**.

## 6. Serious Adverse Events & Safety Reporting
- 24-hour expedited reporting of all SAEs to Sponsor Safety.
- Potential Hy's Law candidate monitoring mandatory.
